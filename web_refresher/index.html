Traditional Italian Level 5 Project

Artisan Margherita Pizza

Wood-fired style, blistered bubbly crust, sweet San Marzano tomatoes, and creamy buffalo mozzarella.

⏱️
Prep Time 20 mins
🔥
Bake Time 12 mins
⭐
Difficulty Beginner

Ingredients

Fresh & Simple
Servings: 2
  • 🌱 250g Pizza Flour (Tipo 00)
  • 💧 160ml Lukewarm Water
  • 🍞 4g Active Dry Yeast
  • 🧀 100g Fresh Mozzarella
  • 🍅 80g San Marzano Tomatoes
  • 🌿 6 Fresh Basil Leaves
💡 Chef's Secret: Let your stretched dough rest for 5 minutes before topping to prevent shrinking in the oven.

Step-by-Step Method

6 Steps
  1. 01

    Bloom the Yeast

    Dissolve the active dry yeast into lukewarm water. Let it stand undisturbed for 5 minutes until frothy and aromatic.

  2. 02

    Knead to Windowpane

    Combine flour and the yeast mixture. Knead vigorously on a clean surface for 8–10 minutes until smooth, glossy, and elastic.

  3. 03

    The First Rise

    Shape into a smooth ball, coat lightly in extra virgin olive oil, and rest covered in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled in volume.

  4. 04

    Blistering Oven Pre-heat

    Crank your oven to its highest setting (240°C / 475°F or higher). If using a pizza stone or steel, pre-heat it for 45 minutes.

  5. 05

    Shape & Dress

    Hand-stretch the dough outward preserving the airy rim. Spoon crushed tomatoes lightly and scatter hand-torn mozzarella pieces.

  6. 06

    Bake & Garnish

    Bake 10–12 minutes until crust forms charred leopard spots and cheese bubbles. Top with fresh whole basil leaves and drizzle olive oil.
